My Fitness Routine

Morning Yoga

Start your day with 20 minutes of yoga to improve flexibility and calm your mind.

Cardio Workout

Do 30 minutes of running, cycling, or skipping to boost heart health.

Strength Training

Focus on muscles with push-ups, squats, and weight training exercises.

Evening Walk

A relaxing 20-minute walk helps digestion and reduces stress.

Stretching

Stretch your body to avoid injuries and improve flexibility.

Hydration

Drink enough water throughout the day to stay energized.
