Facts about dogs

Dogs can hear sounds four times farther away than humans and detect high-pitched noises beyond human range. Their noses are wet to help absorb scent chemicals. While they do see in color, it is limited to shades of blue, yellow, and gray, but they are very sensitive to movement.

All dogs are descendants of a now-extinct Eurasian wolf species. The first "true" dog, Cynodictis, existed 30 to 40 million years ago, evolving from a weasel-like mammal called Miacis.
