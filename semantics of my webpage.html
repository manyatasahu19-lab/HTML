Facts about Ai

Early "Thinking" Machines: Before 1956, Alan Turing published "Computing Machinery and Intelligence" (1950), proposing the Turing Test to measure machine intelligence.

Milestone Victories: A major turning point occurred in 1997 when IBM's Deep Blue beat world chess champion Garry Kasparov. Later, in 2016, DeepMind's AlphaGo defeated Go champion Lee Sedol, demonstrating AI's ability to handle highly complex strategic tasks.
